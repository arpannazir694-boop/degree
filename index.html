Uncompromising Standards

The Anatomy of a Degree Bag

Praised by leather masters for our durable construction, hand-binded edges, and zero synthetic fillers.

01

Full-Grain Italian Hides

We use only the outermost layer of the hide, where natural grain density is highest. Unlike corrected or 'genuine' leather, our hides are never sanded down or coated in plastic paint.

02

Traditional Binded Edges

Instead of cheap rubber edge paint that cracks after a single season, Degree crafts bags with double-folded, skived leather binding—the labor-intensive technique championed in historic saddlery.

03

Solid Cast Brass Fittings

Every buckle, swivel clip, and push-lock is forged from solid brass and antique finished. No hollow zinc alloys that snap under pressure.

About The House

Quiet Luxury, Born From Generational Craft

Degree was founded on a singular conviction: genuine luxury should be measured by tactile truth, generational craft, and quiet permanence—never by loud logos.

Studying the slow, natural alchemy of Tuscan tanneries, we craft heirloom pieces from raw hides infused with organic chestnut and mimosa barks over 60 days. With hand-folded binded edges and zero plastic fillers, each piece is designed to develop a rich golden patina over decades.
