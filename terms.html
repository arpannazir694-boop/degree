Article II

Artisanal Craftsmanship & Natural Hide Variations

Every Degree creation is individually crafted using full-grain Tuscan vegetable-tanned hides. Unlike synthetic or heavily corrected leather, genuine full-grain hides exhibit natural characteristics:

  • Subtle variations in grain texture, growth marks, and tonal depth.
  • Gradual darkening and golden patina development through natural sun exposure and handling.
  • Hand-painted edge coatings and hand-finished saddle stitching which may show microscopic artisanal nuances.

Authenticity Note: Natural surface variations are not defects; they are the definitive signature of authentic, uncorrected full-grain leather.

Article IV

White-Glove Shipping & Consignment Dispatch

Each Degree creation is nestled within a protective cotton dust bag and presented in an archival rigid gift box before dispatch:

  • Domestic Delivery (India): Complimentary insured express delivery on all orders exceeding ₹12,000. Transit typically requires 2 to 4 business days.
  • International Delivery: Dispatched via DHL Express with full tracking and signature verification. Import duties are computed at destination according to national customs guidelines.
  • Transit Insurance: All consignments are fully insured until physical delivery to your designated address is completed.
Article V

14-Day Quiet Luxury Returns & Exchanges

We want you to treasure your Degree piece for a lifetime. Should you wish to return or exchange an unmonogrammed piece, we offer a seamless 14-day return window from the date of recorded delivery.

To qualify for a full refund:

  • The item must be in entirely unused, pristine condition with protective film intact.
  • All original packaging, certificates of authenticity, and dust bags must be returned in their original state.
  • Returns must be initiated through the Orders & Consignment Portal or by contacting Concierge.
Article VI

Lifetime Stitch & Structural Warranty

Degree provides an unconditional Lifetime Stitch Guarantee. We stand behind our traditional saddle stitching, bridle rivets, and solid brass hardware.

Should any seam or hardware component require restoration due to craftsmanship failure, our master artisans will re-stitch or restore your bag at our atelier free of charge. Normal wear and tear, intentional misuse, or exposure to harsh solvents are excluded.
